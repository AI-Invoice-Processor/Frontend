# AI Invoice Processor — Enterprise Visual Identity v1

Assets are independent editable vector SVG files and corresponding rendered PNG files. No NEPXA logo or naming.

## Brand foundations

- Primary name: **AI Invoice Processor**
- Visual positioning: enterprise B2B, calm, trusted, precise, structured.
- Primary palette: Deep Navy `#10243D`, Midnight `#0A182B`, Charcoal `#263545`
- Supporting palette: Steel Blue `#768BA0`, Corporate Blue `#355F8B`, Cool Gray `#627286`
- Canvas: `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`; outlines `#D8E0E8`
- Typeface in SVG: Inter with sane fallbacks (fonts are not packaged).
- Logomark: abstract AI monogram using four sharply structured geometric ribbons; no literal document or sparkle.

## Recommended use

- Top navigation on white: `01_Logos/SVG/logo-horizontal-primary-transparent.svg`
- Navigation on navy: `01_Logos/SVG/logo-horizontal-white-transparent.svg`
- Small marks: `02_Favicon_Avatar/PNG/favicon-transparent.png`, `logo-symbol-primary-transparent.svg`
- Landing page hero: `03_Web_Backgrounds/PNG/hero-white-1920x980.png`
- Dark institutional section: `03_Web_Backgrounds/PNG/hero-navy-1920x980.png`
- LinkedIn banner: `04_Social_Media/PNG/linkedin-banner-white-1584x396.png`
- Open Graph social preview: `04_Social_Media/PNG/open-graph-white-1200x630.png`
- Feature illustrations: `05_Web_Illustrations/`
- Action pictograms: `06_UI_Icons/`

SVGs use editable vector geometry and live text. If your software does not have Inter, text may substitute. Outline text in your preferred design app before sending to print. This is a design proposal, not trademark clearance.

All images are separate assets, not one combined collage.
